Neurodiversity Awareness Month

A month-long programme designed and delivered inside Datavant, built in partnership with the Learning & Development function and local Ireland HR: five newsletters, two live sessions and a suite of practical tools. The aim throughout was to move past awareness content toward small, workable changes in how the organisation runs day to day.

Shortlisted — Workplace Equity Initiative Award, Diversity Inclusion Tech Awards 2026, in partnership with Dublin Tech Summit. Winners announced 17 September 2026 in Dublin.
